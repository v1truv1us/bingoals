Introduction

Bingoals is a goal tracking application that helps users organize and achieve their objectives through a bingo board interface. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

Local Data Only

Current Version: Bingoals currently stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

No Personal Information Collection

We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Any information that could identify you personally

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All your data is stored locally on your device using device's secure storage mechanisms
  • No Cloud Sync: Current version does not synchronize data to external servers
  • Encryption: Data is protected by device-level security features

Legal

This privacy policy is designed to comply with:

  • Apple App Store Guidelines
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Other applicable privacy regulations

Bottom Line: Your data stays on your device. Always.
